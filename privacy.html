Privacy Concerns

Automated license plate reader cameras are designed to photograph vehicles and identify license plate numbers. These systems can help law enforcement locate vehicles connected to investigations, but they can also raise privacy concerns.

One concern is that a license plate reader can record when and where a vehicle was seen. When information from many cameras is combined, it may create a record of a vehicle's movements over time.

This creates questions about how much location information should be collected about people who are not suspected of committing a crime. Communities may also want to understand what protections exist to prevent this information from being misused.

Questions to Consider

  • Who should be allowed to search the information?
