What Are Flock Cameras?

Flock Block is an informational website created to help people understand automated license plate reader technology and the concerns surrounding its use.

Why Are People Concerned?

Privacy

Automated license plate readers can collect information about vehicles traveling through public areas. This raises questions about privacy and how collected information is used.

Data Collection

Another important issue is understanding what information is collected, how long it is stored, and who may have access to it.

Transparency and Oversight

Communities may also want to know how these systems are approved, monitored, and regulated.
