Data Collection

Automated license plate readers collect information when vehicles pass cameras located along roads, near businesses, or in other public locations.

Information collected may include a photograph of the vehicle, the license plate number, the date and time the vehicle was detected, and the location of the camera.

An important concern is what happens to this information after it is collected. People may want to know how long the information is stored, where it is stored, and whether it can be shared with other agencies.

ABC News: Flock Cameras Face Nationwide Backlash Over Privacy Concerns

An ABC News article published on September 1, 2026, discusses growing concerns about Flock cameras across the United States. The article explains how automated license plate readers collect vehicle information and how that data can be used by law enforcement.

The article also discusses concerns about surveillance data being misused, how long information should be stored, and who should be allowed to access it. While supporters believe the cameras can help solve crimes, critics question how the information is collected and monitored.

This article provides another example of why data collection policies, privacy protections, and accountability are important when using surveillance technology.
