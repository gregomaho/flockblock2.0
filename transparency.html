Transparency and Oversight

Transparency is important when surveillance technology is used in a community. Residents may want to know where cameras are located, why they are being used, and what policies control their use.

Oversight can include rules explaining who may access collected information, when searches are permitted, and how the system is monitored for misuse.

Communities may also want opportunities to discuss surveillance technology before new systems are purchased or expanded. Public policies and clear documentation can help residents better understand how the technology is being used.

Questions About Oversight

  • Who approves the installation of cameras?
  • Are camera locations publicly available?
  • Are searches of the system documented?
  • Who investigates possible misuse?
  • Can residents review policies governing the system?
